# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-08_

**OneBreath** ("the app") is designed with privacy as a core principle. This document explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app does and does not handle.

## TL;DR
OneBreath collects **nothing**. All your data stays on your device. The app makes **no network requests**.

## What data the app stores
The app stores the following information **locally on your device** using the operating system's standard secure storage (`AsyncStorage` on iOS / Android, `localStorage` on web):

- Your breath-hold session history (timestamp and duration of each hold)
- Your personal best
- Any in-app preferences you set (e.g. breathe-up settings)

This data never leaves your device. It is not synced to any cloud, server, or third party. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## What data the app does NOT collect
- ❌ No personal information (no name, email, account, or login)
- ❌ No analytics or telemetry
- ❌ No advertising identifiers
- ❌ No crash reporting that includes user data
- ❌ No location data
- ❌ No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- ❌ No third-party tracking SDKs

## Network usage
OneBreath does not make any network requests during normal use. The app functions fully offline.

## Children's privacy
Because OneBreath collects no personal data of any kind, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Third-party services
OneBreath uses the following open-source libraries that run entirely on-device and do not transmit data: Expo, React Native, react-native-reanimated, react-native-svg, react-native-gifted-charts, expo-haptics, expo-keep-awake, AsyncStorage. None of these are configured to send data off-device in OneBreath.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in the app's GitHub repository at https://github.com/william051200/OneBreath with a new "Last updated" date.
